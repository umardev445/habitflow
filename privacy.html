Overview

📌 Quick Summary:

HabitFlow Pro is designed with privacy-first principles. We do NOT sell your data, we do NOT share your personal information with third parties, and all your habit data stays on YOUR device unless you choose to export it.

🌍 Our Commitment

At HabitFlow Pro, we believe that your personal habit data is sensitive and private. Our app is built on the principle of data minimization and local-first storage. This means:

  • ✅ Your data never leaves your browser unless you explicitly export it
  • ✅ No external servers store your habits or personal information
  • ✅ No tracking pixels or analytics scripts
  • ✅ Complete transparency about how the app works

🏢 Who We Are

HabitFlow Pro is a personal habit tracking application created to help individuals build better habits. We operate as a client-side only application, meaning all processing happens directly in your web browser.

Data Collection

📝 What Data We Collect

HabitFlow Pro collects only the data you actively provide:

  • Habit Information: Habit names, categories, and completion dates
  • Usage Preferences: App settings (dark mode, notification preferences)
  • No Personal Identifiers: We don't collect names, emails, IP addresses, or location data

💾 How Data is Stored

All data is stored locally in your browser using the localStorage API. This means:

  • 📀 Data persists between browser sessions
  • 🌐 No internet connection required for the app to work
  • 🔐 Only you can access your data (no cloud storage)
  • 🗑️ You can clear data at any time via browser settings or app reset
⚠️ Important Note:

Clearing your browser's cache/localStorage will permanently delete all your habit data. We recommend using the export feature to create backups.

📤 Data Export & Sharing

You have complete control over your data:

  • ✅ Export your data as JSON file anytime
  • ✅ Import previously exported backups
  • ✅ Delete all data permanently via Settings
  • ❌ We do NOT have access to your exported files

Cookie Policy

🍪 What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember information about your visit.

🔧 How HabitFlow Pro Uses localStorage

Instead of traditional cookies, we use the browser's localStorage feature which serves a similar purpose but with more control:

  • Essential Data: Stores your habits, completions, and app preferences
  • No Tracking Cookies: We don't use any third-party cookies or tracking mechanisms
  • Session Persistence: Keeps your data available between visits
🔐 Your Cookie Choices:

You can disable localStorage in your browser settings, but this will prevent the app from saving your data. You can also clear localStorage anytime via browser developer tools or the app's reset feature.

📊 Third-Party Services

HabitFlow Pro uses NO third-party services that set cookies or track users. The only external resource is Chart.js (for graphs), which doesn't set any cookies or collect data.

Your Privacy Rights

🌐 GDPR & CCPA Compliance

As a client-side application with no server-side data processing, HabitFlow Pro inherently respects your privacy rights:

  • Right to Access: View all your data through the app interface
  • Right to Delete: Delete individual habits or reset all data
  • Right to Portability: Export your data in JSON format
  • Right to Object: No data processing to object to since everything is local
  • Right to Rectification: Edit your habits and completions anytime

🛡️ How to Exercise Your Rights

These actions perform the requested operations immediately. No waiting period or verification required since all data is under your control.

👶 Children's Privacy

HabitFlow Pro does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and doesn't require any age verification since no personal data is collected.

User Consent

✅ By Using This App, You Consent To:

  • ✓ Local storage of your habit data in your browser
  • ✓ Using localStorage for app functionality
  • ✓ Exporting your data (when you choose to do so)
  • ✓ App settings being saved locally

📋 What You DON'T Consent To:

  • ✗ Your data being sent to any external servers
  • ✗ Third-party tracking or analytics
  • ✗ Your personal information being sold or shared
  • ✗ Unauthorized access to your data
🔄 Withdrawing Consent:

You can withdraw your consent at any time by:

  1. Clearing your browser's localStorage/cache
  2. Using the "Reset All Data" button in Settings
  3. Simply stopping use of the application

📧 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. The latest version will always be available within the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
